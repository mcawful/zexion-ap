## What's new

- **Two new traps, Disruption Trap and Disorient Trap,** take the place of some of your Scrap (15% by default: usually 2 or 3 in a seed, fewer or none with the OS Install Disks goal, since the disks take the Scrap first).
- **Disruption Trap:** what a Phazo's touch does, for about 12 seconds. Your energy weapons don't fire and your charge stays empty; missiles and Mega Bombs still work.
- **Disorient Trap:** what a Balorb's wave does, for about 12 seconds. Your controls are reversed.
- **Traps are on in a YAML you already have.** Set `trap_percentage: 0` for none, or raise it for more; `disruption_trap_weight` and `disorient_trap_weight` pick the mix.
- **A trap looks like an important item.** A Zexion player's trap looks like one of that player's upgrades, keys or OS Install Disks; another game's trap looks like a progression item. You can't tell it from the real thing until you pick it up, and one that looks like a disk never counts as one.
- **Traps need the mod.** Without it a trap does nothing, one sent to you or your own, and the client says so. Your own traps go off the moment you pick them up, even with the client closed, as long as the mod is installed.
- **A trap goes off as its popup shows**, after the popups already waiting, such as a disk's and its count. It waits through menus, a death and a load, and goes off once you are back in play.
- **A teleport from the pause menu holds things back until you arrive.** A trap or an Energy Shield sent to you comes once you get there (checked in the game with TELEPORT TO START). Popups, Scrap drops and DeathLink wait the same way (not yet checked in the game).
- **Other players' items stay found.** With the client connected, an item for another player that the server has as checked stays gone from its room after a reload, and when its owner collects it with `!collect`. A kept item gives you no Scrap Metal (picking one up still does).
- **In an upside-down room,** the mod also hides the leftover picture of a kept item.
- **Three rooms are left out:** Exodia 20,0, R'Talva 34,51 and Kresezoa 5,23, where picking the item up is what moves the game on or the boss still drops it. Once their item is checked, Scrap Metal lies there instead (with the mod, the game's own Scrap Metal), and you still pick it up.
- **Your own OS Install Disks and traps follow your save.** If you reload without saving after picking one up, it is back in its room: pick it up again and a disk counts again, a trap goes off again.
- **Your disk count is your save's own.** It drops when a reload puts one of your own disks back in its room. It shows after each disk's popup, on the pause screen and on the file select (not yet checked in the game as it is now), and with the client closed, when you pick up one of your own.
- **Area Maps stay in your own world** when Area Map Hints is off, since picking one up reveals the map around the square it lay in. An item plando that sends one to another player isn't placed, and the generator warns.
- **New client messages** say when it can't mark other players' items collected, and that traps do nothing without the mod. Troubleshooting in the setup guide has what to do.

## Fixes

- ARCHIPELAGO CLIENT CONNECTED. now shows before the popups of items that arrived while you were away, not after them.
- The guides no longer suggest you may need to visit Vallahar 6,8 before the scanner at Guardia 42,26 will tell you how many disks you need.
- When a very large batch of items reaches you at once, its popups now come in several batches instead of being lost (not yet checked in the game).
