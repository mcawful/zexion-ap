The first release: play Zexion in an Archipelago multiworld.

## Features

- **Start a seed from the game's own menu:** pick an empty save slot and choose ARCHIPELAGO.
- **Items for other players** show the Archipelago logo and who they're for. Items you receive
  pop up with who sent them.
- **Two goals:** reach the ending, or collect enough OS Install Disks and return home from the
  Landing Pod.
- **Every mode:** Easy, Standard, OHKO and Custom, with all of the randomizer's settings in your
  YAML.
- **Intel Chips:** Area Maps can hint where your important items are.
- **DeathLink.**
- **Play without the client:** your seed's saves keep their look, and your checks are sent the
  next time you connect.
- **Your Feats and Steam achievements stay clean:** none are earned in Archipelago saves. Your
  normal saves are untouched.
- **Languages:** made for the default English and English(HD). Other languages work, with the
  mod's own text in English.

## Installing

The full steps are in the
[setup guide](https://github.com/mcawful/zexion-ap/blob/v1.0.0/worlds/zexion/docs/setup_en.md),
also attached below as `zexion-setup-guide.md`. You need Zexion on Steam (Windows), Steam build
23943567, and Archipelago 0.6.8 or newer.

1. In the Archipelago Launcher, choose **Install APWorld** and pick `zexion.apworld`.
2. Close Zexion and copy `zexion_ap.dll` and `version.dll` into its folder (in Steam:
   right-click Zexion, Manage, Browse local files).
3. Make your YAML from the sample for your mode (`zexion-standard.yaml` and the others), then
   generate and host as usual.
4. Start Zexion, open **Zexion Client** from the Launcher and connect.
5. In Zexion, pick an empty save slot and choose **ARCHIPELAGO**.

Something wrong? See Troubleshooting in the setup guide, or report it at
[github.com/mcawful/zexion-ap/issues](https://github.com/mcawful/zexion-ap/issues) with the
client's log.
