## What's new

- **See who sent you a disk or an Intel Chip.** An OS Install Disk or Intel Chip from another player now pops up with their name first, then your disk count or the chip's hint.
- **Scrap from other players is useful now.** Scrap Metal, Scrap Energy and Scrap Chunk sent to you drop the same pickups the game's own scrap does.
- **No more flickering messages.** Long, four- or five-line messages open and close cleanly.
- **Another Zexion player's scrap looks like scrap** in your world, instead of an oddly colored Archipelago logo.
- **Clearer item plando.** If a plando asks for something the game can't do, the generator now says what and why, instead of making a seed that won't play right. See "Can I use plando?" on the game page.

## Fixes

- An Intel Chip you receive while the client is disconnected now still shows its hint.
- When you find a disk just as one arrives from another player, each popup now shows its own count, in order.
- The guides now give the scanner's location as Guardia 42,26.
