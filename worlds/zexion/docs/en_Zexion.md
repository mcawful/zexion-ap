# Zexion

## What does randomization do to this game?

Zexion has its own randomizer. This world uses its map and its rules for what each item opens
up, but lets Archipelago decide what is at each of the game's 154 item locations. Items found
in Zexion can belong to other players, and their games can send items to you.

## What is the goal?

Grabbing Zexion in the core, which takes the Alpha, Beta, Gamma and Omega Keys. This is the
moment the game's own "Abridged Finale" setting counts as victory; the escape, the final battle
and the ending after it are not needed.

## Which modes are there?

The Mode option takes the game's own Easy or Standard mode, with that mode's item pool,
starting items and settings. OHKO is not offered yet.

## Where does the game start?

In one of the game's own start rooms, chosen with the Start Room option. Only rooms from which
the mode's starting items reach a few locations are offered.

## What does another world's item look like in Zexion?

Collecting a location gives you a placeholder item in the game, and the companion client sends
the real item to its owner. Items for you arrive while you play, without the usual popup.
